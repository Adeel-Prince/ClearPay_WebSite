About ClearPay

We believe payments should be understandable

ClearPay started with a simple frustration: nobody could explain what a payment actually cost.

Our mission

We build payment tools that show every fee, move money quickly and protect your customers. Our team combines experience from banking, e-commerce and open-source software.

ClearPay is a fictional startup created as a learning project, to practise building a complete, accessible and responsive website.

  • Clarity

    Plain language and visible numbers.

  • Trust

    Security is never an optional extra.

  • Inclusion

    Accessible to everyone, on every device.

Our story so far

  1. 2022Two friends sketch a payment dashboard that explains every fee.
  2. 2023First 500 businesses join the private beta.
  3. 2024Instant payouts and the developer API launch.
  4. 2025ClearPay passes 18,000 businesses and opens its fraud shield to all plans.
  5. 2026A new fully accessible website and pricing calculator go live.
