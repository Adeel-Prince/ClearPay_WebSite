Pricing questions

Are there setup or hidden fees?

No. You pay the plan fee (if any), the card rate and any add-ons you switch on. The calculator above includes all of them.

How is the "best plan" chosen?

We calculate the total monthly cost of every plan for your inputs and highlight the cheapest one.

What counts as an international card?

Any card issued outside your business country. It adds 1% to those payments.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can switch plans at any time and the change applies from your next billing period.
